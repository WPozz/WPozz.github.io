# 🧠 William Pozzolini — Academic Portfolio & Research CV

## 🔬 About Me & Research Overview

I am a Bioengineer with passion for AI, Tech and Research. Find more about me at https://wpozz.github.io/


### Core Research Pillars
- **Medical AI & Self-Supervised Learning**: Joint-Embedding Architectures (C-JEPA), variance-invariance-covariance regularizations (VICReg), Vision Transformers (ViT).
- **High-Performance Computing (HPC)**: Distributed multi-GPU deep learning on Linux clusters orchestrated via SLURM and CUDA Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP).
- **Neuroengineering & BCI**: Real-time EEG bandpower processing, online motor imagery decoding in ROS, and wearable biosensor integrations.
- **Computational Biomechanics**: Anatomical 3D ROI segmentation (ITK-SNAP), finite element analysis (FEA), and stress modeling in COMSOL Multiphysics.
